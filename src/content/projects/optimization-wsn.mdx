This research analyzes the factors affecting **wireless-sensor-network (WSN)**
and **smart-grid** network lifetime — including transmission power control,
node-failure and attack scenarios, and routing strategies — through
(mixed-)integer programming models.

### Details

<img src="/images/projects/wsn.jpg" alt="Wireless sensor network lifetime optimization" class="float-left" />

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) are widely used in border security, military
applications, power grids, critical infrastructures and smart spaces, and thus,
they are naturally attractive to the adversaries and vulnerable to challenging
environmental conditions. In general, after constructing a sensor network, its
maintenance may not be feasible due to their harsh topologies. Hence, a WSN
should be designed by considering many different factors, which impact the
network's lifetime. Throughout my B.Sc. and M.Sc., I analyzed many of these
factors, and studied on the design. More specifically, I implemented and
[open-sourced various models in (mixed-) integer programming frameworks](https://github.com/euzun/SensorNetworks)
for measuring the impact of following factors and scenarios on the lifetime of WSNs.

- **Sensor specs:** Transmission power control strategies between sensor nodes.
- **Attack scenarios:** Elimination of the most critical node on the network.
- **Routing and topology:** Scalable routing scenarios for different network topologies.
