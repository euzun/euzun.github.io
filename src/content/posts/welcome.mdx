Welcome to my new personal website! This is a space where I'll be sharing my work in cryptography, privacy-preserving systems, authentication at scale, and applied engineering. You'll find technical deep-dives on my published research, engineering insights I've learned building large-scale systems in industry, and longer-form essays on topics I find interesting.

This site is built with Astro 5 and Tailwind CSS — it's fast, static, and completely portable. Every page is optimized for reading, with a focus on typography and clarity. I'm using this as my primary platform for technical writing going forward.

**More content coming soon.** Blog posts, research updates, and project retrospectives will be published here regularly. You can follow along via RSS.
